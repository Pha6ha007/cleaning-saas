## Data & Migration (Hard Boundary)

CleanProof does **not** migrate historical data.

This is a deliberate product decision.

### What this means

- CleanProof **does not import** past jobs, photos, checklists, or messages
  from Excel, WhatsApp, or other tools.
- The pilot **always starts with new jobs only**.
- Existing Excel files and WhatsApp chats remain where they are.
- CleanProof becomes the **source of truth from today forward**, not a rebuild of the past.

### Why this rule exists

CleanProof is a proof layer, not a data warehouse.

Historical records created without:
- GPS verification,
- enforced checklists,
- controlled photo capture,
- and immutable audit trails

cannot be retroactively turned into proof.

Importing such data would create a **false sense of verification**, which
directly contradicts the product’s purpose.

### Allowed (future, optional)

At a later stage, we may support a **simple one-time import** of reference data only:

- Locations (name, address)
- Cleaners (name, contact)

This import is:
- manual (template-based),
- limited to setup convenience,
- never includes job history or evidence.

### How we communicate this to customers

**Standard response:**

> CleanProof doesn’t replace your existing tools for historical records.  
> We start from today and forward.
>
> Your Excel files and WhatsApp chats stay as they are.  
> All new jobs are planned and proven in CleanProof.
>
> The pilot goal is simple:  
> see if verified proof reduces disputes better than your current process.

### Qualification rule

If a company requires:
- full migration from Excel,
- WhatsApp history import,
- or rebuilding past jobs

as a condition to start,

**we politely decline**.

Such companies are not a fit for CleanProof at this stage.
